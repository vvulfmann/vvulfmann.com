Privacy

This site is a single page about my games. There are no accounts, forms, or ads here.

Visitor counts

I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see roughly how many people visit, which pages they look at, what kind of device they use, their country, and which site sent them here. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across other sites, and doesn't tell me who you are. Nothing is sold or used for ads.

Things saved in your browser

The site remembers your music volume and whether you paused the music, in your own browser. That never leaves your device.
